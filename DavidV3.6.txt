4. cognitive_core/spatial_sketchbook.py (NEW)

""" spatial_sketchbook.py — David v3.6 [NEW] The Spatial Sketchbook implements Hierarchical Video Memory (Improvement 4). Design: - Every SNAPSHOT_INTERVAL_S (10s), WorldModel calls queue_snapshot(). - A background worker thread pops snapshots, computes a sentence-transformer embedding of a short scene description (generated via a fast caption pass), and stores (embedding, 3D anchor points, timestamp, frame path) in ChromaDB. - At query time, the Planner calls retrieve_anchors(query, k) to get the k most semantically similar anchors — including their 3D coordinates — enabling "where is the tool I just set down?" even after 60 seconds have passed.
